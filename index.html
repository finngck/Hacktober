How it works

Three steps to a new plan.

  1. Check in How long did you sleep, and how much energy do you have today?
  2. Say what’s going on “Missed my train”, “meeting moved”, “totally wiped” – in your own words.
  3. Apply the proposal A new schedule with energy tips. Apply it, discard it or undo it.
